MacCalendar @VERSION@

A menu bar calendar with a dual world clock and flags for macOS.

The app is installed in Applications. When the installation finishes, MacCalendar opens and its icon appears in the menu bar; it also starts by itself whenever you log in (you can turn this off in Settings).

Requires macOS 13 or later. Runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
